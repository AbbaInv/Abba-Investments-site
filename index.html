Meet Eric Summers

Hi, I'm Eric. I'm a licensed Florida real estate professional with Avanti Way Realty and the founder of Abba Investments.

I got into real estate because I've seen what a stable home does for a person — and what losing one can do. Addiction, a broken family, a run of bad luck… a safe place to land, with structure and people who care, can be the turning point.

So we run a sharp, honest real estate business — and use it to give back through Section 8 housing and faith-based transitional homes. You'll get straight talk, real numbers, and someone who picks up the phone.

FAQ

Do you accept Section 8?Yes — we welcome Housing Choice Voucher holders.
How fast can you buy my house?Often within a couple of weeks, depending on title — or on your schedule.
Will a cash offer be less than listing?Usually, yes. We show you both numbers so you can choose.
What is "subject-to"?Buying a home while the seller's existing mortgage stays in place. It can help in tough spots but carries risks; we explain it in writing and recommend an attorney.
